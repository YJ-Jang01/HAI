# Study

## Study Goal

Evaluate whether in-place AI evidence overlays help users compare multiple products with less navigation cost and better evidence awareness than baseline browsing or chat-only AI help.

## Research Questions

1. Does in-place evidence reduce detail-page openings and task time?
2. Does spatially attached evidence improve item-evidence matching and decision justification?
3. Does showing source snippets improve trust calibration and perceived control?

## Conditions

### C1: Baseline Browsing

Users browse the grid and open product detail pages.

### C2: Chat-Only AI

AI summaries appear in a separate chat/panel, not attached to cards.

### C3: In-Place Evidence Overlay

AI evidence appears on selected product cards.

### C4: In-Place Overlay With Provenance

Overlays include expandable review snippets and repair/undo.

For a course project, C1-C3 may be enough.

## Example Tasks

- Choose the best wireless headphones under a budget for commuting.
- Find the keyboard with low noise and good typing feel.
- Pick a product with few durability complaints.
- Compare three similar products and justify your final choice.
- Find which product has the strongest negative reviews about battery life.

## Metrics

- task completion time
- number of detail modal opens
- number of selected products
- number of repairs
- number of snippet expansions
- final choice quality
- justification quality
- confidence
- perceived control
- trust in AI
- workload

## Procedure

1. Consent and short intro.
2. Practice task.
3. Main tasks across conditions.
4. Post-task questionnaire.
5. Final short interview.

## Prototype Requirements Before Study

- stable product set
- stable task prompts
- condition switch or clear researcher instruction
- working logging/export
- final choice form or equivalent recording method
- pilot with 1-2 users before main study
